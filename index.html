DEVDASH: 3D Hardware Learning Hub

Online hardware tutorials are fragmented and risky. Hardware components are expensive, and wiring mistakes can instantly fry them. We built a one-stop web platform for rookie hardware engineers.

• Interactive 3D Assembly: Features ready-made 3D models with exploded views so rookies can visualize physical assembly step-by-step.
• Schematics & BOM: Follow-along wiring schematics, in-depth component breakdowns, and direct sourcing Bill of Materials (BOM).
Interactive 3D Exploded Views Wiring Schematics BOM Sourcing

Graphicaa: Unveil Cross-Dataset AI

Countless massive open-source datasets exist online, yet valuable insights hide at the intersection of unrelated data (such as road traffic patterns correlated with emergency hospital admissions).

• The Unveil Engine: Users select any 3 distinct datasets. The AI engine vectorizes and analyzes them to surface unintuitive correlations.
• Research Direction: Provides immediate directional hypotheses to guide researchers toward unexplored problem domains.
